# AI Usage Note

I used AI tools as a development partner to inspect the assignment PDF, extract the Option 2 requirements, choose a small client-side architecture, generate the initial React implementation, and review the build errors.

The important product decisions were made deliberately: the flow is Ask → Clarify → Define → Test → Learn; missing assumptions are shown before the experiment; mock data is labeled; and the result separates observations from conclusions. I rejected a generic chatbot layout because the brief evaluates research thinking, not just conversational UI. I also kept the test deterministic so the reviewer can reproduce the journey without credentials or external data.

The part I am most proud of is the clarification step. It makes the ambiguity in “sharp fall” and “works” visible, which keeps the prototype honest and gives the eventual AI system a useful contract to fill.
